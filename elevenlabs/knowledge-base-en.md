## Frequently asked questions
### How do escrow payments work on GoParty?
When you book, you pay through GoParty and the money is held securely in escrow. It is released to the seller only once your party is confirmed, so you never pay a seller directly.

### How are sellers verified?
Every seller passes identity, trade licence and quality checks before their profile goes live, and listings go through multi-layer verification to keep fake listings off GoParty.

### Can I get a refund?
It depends on who cancels and why. If the supplier cancels, you get a full refund and may be entitled to compensation. If you cancel, the refund depends on the published cancellation policy and the costs already incurred. A defective service can be performed again or refunded. Refunds go back to your original payment method when possible.

### My guests did not receive my WhatsApp invitation. What can I do?
WhatsApp restrictions can sometimes stop an invitation from being delivered. In that case GoParty may send it by SMS or another available channel so it still reaches your guests. If a guest is still missing it, check their number and contact support.

### Why was my invitation rejected?
Invitations must follow our publishing guidelines: no vague wording, no political or sectarian purposes, correct guest contact details, and permission from the host if you send on their behalf.

### Can I use more than one discount on a booking?
No. Only one discount or offer can be applied at a time, and GoParty applies the highest or most suitable one.

### How do I become a seller on GoParty?
Choose Join as a Seller, create your company profile and pass verification. Then publish your packages with pricing and availability, and start receiving bookings and escrow payouts.
